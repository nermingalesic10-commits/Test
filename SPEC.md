# TARGET: Internship career journey

Choose the idea, person, interaction, and visual direction. The agent can help phrase and save your decisions after you approve them. The provided scope and review safeguards stay in place.

- **Thing:** Build a one-page static internship and career-journey tracker with two distinct, page-like views: **Applications** for internship/job entries and **Career Map** for a motivating path toward a desired role.
- **Audience:** Students and early-career job seekers ages 18–25 who want to organize applications and track the skills and milestones needed for their dream internship or job.
- **Requirements:** Provide manual application entries, a Career Map styled like a friendly game board, skill-progress cards for Excel, SQL, Python, and related skills, and visible achievement badges for completed milestones. Switching between Applications and Career Map is the primary interaction and must make the selected view and its results understandable.
- **Guardrails:** Static browser code. No required external service, keys, accounts, runtime AI, private data, URL scraping, or AI processing. Clearly label fictional or sample content. Preserve the example and publishing setup. Work on a branch and wait for human review before shipping. A future URL/AI import and personalized-account system may be shown only as clearly labeled planned functionality; each requires separate approval, a secure backend, credential handling, privacy design, and permission-aware integrations.
- **Experience:** Make the site professional, colorful, and user-friendly. Applications and Career Map should feel like separate pages while remaining a single responsive page; the map should feel game-like and motivating through pathways, milestones, and badges without becoming childish or hard to scan.
- **Test:** I can switch between Applications and Career Map, inspect a sample application and its status, select or inspect a career milestone, and see related skill progress, completion state, achievement badge, and the standing rule's effect in the actual preview. After I approve and merge, the same registered Pages URL works.
