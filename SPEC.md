# TARGET: Internship career journey

Choose the idea, person, interaction, and visual direction. The agent can help phrase and save your decisions after you approve them. The provided scope and review safeguards stay in place.

- **Thing:** Build a two-page static internship and career-journey tracker: **Applications** for internship/job entries and **Career Map** for a motivating path toward a desired role.
- **Audience:** Students and early-career job seekers ages 18–25 who want to organize applications and track the skills and milestones needed for their dream internship or job.
- **Requirements:** Provide a manual application tracker on Applications and a Career Map styled like a friendly game board on Career Map. Include skill-progress cards for Excel, SQL, Python, and related skills, plus visible achievement badges for completed milestones. A visitor can navigate between the two pages and select an application or milestone to reveal its related details.
- **Guardrails:** User-approved scope exception: use two static pages rather than one. Otherwise use static browser code only—no required external service, keys, accounts, runtime AI, private data, URL scraping, or AI processing. Clearly label fictional or sample content. Preserve the example and publishing setup. Work on a branch and wait for human review before shipping. A future URL/AI import and personalized-account system may be shown only as clearly labeled planned functionality; each requires separate approval, a secure backend, credential handling, privacy design, and permission-aware integrations.
- **Experience:** Make the site professional, colorful, and user-friendly. Applications and Career Map should feel coherent while presenting distinct purposes; the map should feel game-like and motivating through pathways, milestones, and badges without becoming childish or hard to scan.
- **Test:** I can navigate between Applications and Career Map, add or inspect a sample manual application and its status, select a career milestone, and see related skill progress, completion state, achievement badge, and the standing rule's effect in the actual preview. After I approve and merge, the same registered Pages URL works.
